Frequently asked questions

Does Aperture Studio AI move or alter my files?
No. The catalog is non-destructive: point it at the folders you already have and it indexes everything without copying, moving, or altering a single original. Even edits (crop, rotation) stay in the catalog and are only applied on export.
Does the AI send my photos to the cloud?
Not by default. Keywords, semantic search, and faces run with local models on your PC. Google Cloud Vision and AWS Rekognition are optional, use your own keys, and stay off until you enable them.
Does it work with my camera's RAW files?
Very likely yes: Canon, Nikon, Sony, Fujifilm, Olympus, Panasonic, Pentax, Leica, and DNG, plus HEIC/HEIF from iPhones, JPEG, PNG, TIFF, WebP, and video in MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV, and WebM.
Do I need internet to use it?
Only to activate the app and download media tools on first run. Day-to-day use (catalog, local AI, search, offline geocoding) works without a connection, and the app does not import photos from online services.
